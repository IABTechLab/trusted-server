🛂 Permissions Inspector

Trusted Server never ships a permission policy. The builder of a deployment chooses the permissions.yaml baked into their compiled image, the operator of that image can overlay another, and the visitor's own signals decide the rest at runtime. This page reads such a policy document, written in the IAB Tech Lab Privacy Taxonomy's own vocabulary, and answers the question a policy owner actually has: for a visitor in a given place, with given consent signals, which Data Uses are set? Everything runs in this browser tab. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Awaiting a Privacy Taxonomy addition

The resolution rules are trusted-server's own. The most specific place wins, region then country then the top of the tree. A group states a baseline per Data Use as granted, requires_signal or denied. A TCF record grants the Data Use a consented purpose maps to and revokes a granted one it does not, an opt-out suppresses the Data Uses the policy's revoke list names, even beside a consenting TCF record, and denied always wins. This page is a read-only inspector. A companion editor is planned separately. Inspecting the policy with the same code that enforces it is the point: what you verify here is what runs.
